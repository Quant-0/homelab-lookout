Devices

A device is a physical box: server, NAS, switch, patch panel… Its monitoring connections (iDRAC, Proxmox, TrueNAS, Synology, UniFi) are added on the device itself. Passwords, tokens and keys are encrypted and write-only: only whether one is set is ever shown.

Drag a device to move it. Click it to edit. Double-click an empty U to add a device there.

Power & cost

Used to calculate the cost of the power measured by your iDRACs and switches.

Grid tariff (nettleie), energy part

Copy the day/night energy rates from your grid company’s tariff sheet and tick what they already include.

Fixed monthly costs

Paid regardless of the homelab, so excluded from its cost unless you tick the box. Bigger loads can still move you into a higher capacity step.

Devices without a power sensor

Synology and UniFi devices don't report their own power draw. Estimates are set per device on the Devices tab; PoE power delivered by switches is always measured.
