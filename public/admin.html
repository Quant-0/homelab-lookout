Check-ins

For backups and cron jobs: the job calls its own address when it has run. If it doesn't within the time you set, or reports a failure, you get a Check-in missed alert.

Maintenance

Planned work: problems on what you choose are shown as maintenance, no alerts are sent, and the time doesn't count against uptime.

Dashboard

A public dashboard shows host names and status data to anyone who can reach the server, but never addresses, usernames or secrets. Admin always requires login.

Services on Home

This also applies to the wall display. To leave out single services, untick “Show on Home” on the service. Alert notifications are set on the Alerts tab and are not changed by this.

Power & cost

Used to calculate the cost of the power measured by your iDRACs and switches.

Grid tariff (nettleie), energy part

Copy the day/night energy rates from your grid company’s tariff sheet and tick what they already include.

Fixed monthly costs

Paid regardless of the homelab, so excluded from its cost unless you tick the box. Bigger loads can still move you into a higher capacity step.

Devices without a power sensor

Synology and UniFi devices don't report their own power draw. Estimates are set per device on the Devices tab; PoE power delivered by switches is always measured.

Download or restore a backup

A backup contains settings, devices with their credentials, services, docs, energy history and uploaded images, encrypted with a passphrase you choose. Keep the passphrase somewhere safe: without it the backup cannot be opened.

Admin password

Signed-in browsers

Where you are signed in. A normal sign-in lasts 12 hours (2 without use); "Keep me signed in" 30 days (a week without use). Either way, backups, backup and alert destinations, the web server and making things public ask for the password again after 15 minutes. Changing the password signs out all but this one.

Remembered devices

Where "Remember this device for viewing" was ticked at sign-in. They can view the dashboard, services, energy, docs and updates for 30 days from then; changing anything asks for the password. A new password signs out all but this one.

Web server

How the dashboard is reached. A new port is opened before the old one is closed, so a port that can't be used never takes the dashboard offline.

Ports below 1024 (e.g. 443) need: sudo sh deploy/install.sh --low-ports. Locked out? sudo sh deploy/install.sh --reset-network

Install as an app

The dashboard can be installed on a phone or PC and then opens in its own window with its own icon. The device has to trust the certificate above first (import the CA once), otherwise the browser doesn't offer to install it.

  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Install app.
  • iPhone/iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Windows, macOS, Linux (Chrome, Edge): the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Only the offline screen is kept on the device, nothing from the dashboard. To stay signed in on a phone, tick "Remember this device for viewing" when signing in from the app.
